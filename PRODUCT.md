## Stack

## Users

- People in Bogotá and the Sabana Norte (Chía, Cajicá, Zipaquirá), and elsewhere in Colombia by video call, who bought or are buying a home "sobre planos" through a fiduciaria and a constructora, and the project is late, changed, or the money is stuck in the fiducia.
- People about to sign a promesa de compraventa, an escritura pública, a lease or a real-estate deal who want a lawyer to read or draft it before they sign.
- Owners, possessors and landlords with a property conflict already under way: pertenencia, reivindicatorio, restitución de inmueble arrendado, servidumbres.
- They usually arrive from Google (search / Google Maps) on a phone, worried about a large sum of money, and want to know whether this lawyer handles exactly their problem and how to talk to him.

## Product Purpose

Website for "Miguel Bermúdez – Abogado", the practice of abogado Miguel Bermúdez: real-estate law, advisory, and judicial and extrajudicial representation. Success = a visitor with a real-estate problem writes on WhatsApp or books a consultation (in person or virtual).

## Positioning

A real-estate lawyer with 16 years of professional experience in judicial and extrajudicial representation of people and companies. He works both preventively (advice and contracts before signing) and when the conflict has already started (claims against constructoras and fiduciarias, property and lease litigation), looking for effective solutions that defend the client's patrimony.

## Product Principles

1. Say plainly which problem he takes; a buyer with a stuck apartment or an owner with a property conflict must recognise his case in seconds.
1b. Show both sides of the work: preventive advice, and solving a conflict that already started.
2. Never promise results; legal advertising in Colombia must be truthful and sober.
3. Every path ends in a WhatsApp conversation or a booked consultation.
4. Explain the process (what happens after the first message) instead of claiming excellence.
