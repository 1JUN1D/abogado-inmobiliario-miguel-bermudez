## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, published on GitHub Pages (confirmed by the user, same as the agency's other client sites). Domain not decided yet.

## Users

- People in Bogotá (and elsewhere in Colombia, served by video call) who bought or are buying a home "sobre planos" through a fiduciaria and a constructora, and the project is late, changed, or the money is stuck in the fiducia.
- People about to sign a promesa de compraventa, an escritura pública or a real-estate deal who want a lawyer to read it before they sign.
- They usually arrive from Google (search / Google Maps) on a phone, worried about a large sum of money, and want to know whether this lawyer handles exactly their problem and how to talk to him.

## Product Purpose

Website for Bermúdez Abogados y Asociados, the practice of abogado Miguel Bermúdez: real-estate law, advisory, and judicial and extrajudicial representation. Success = a visitor with a real-estate problem writes on WhatsApp or books a consultation (in person or virtual).

## Positioning

A real-estate lawyer who stands on the buyer's side: he represents the consumidor financiero against fiduciarias and the consumidor inmobiliario against constructoras, in and out of court, besides reviewing contracts and escrituras before signing.

## Product Principles

1. Say plainly which problem he takes; a buyer with a stuck apartment must recognise his case in seconds.
2. Never promise results; legal advertising in Colombia must be truthful and sober.
3. Every path ends in a WhatsApp conversation or a booked consultation.
4. Explain the process (what happens after the first message) instead of claiming excellence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
